### Method 1
```python
n = 5

space = n - 1

# Upper half
for i in range(1, n + 1):
    print(" " * space + "* " * i)
    space -= 1

# Lower half
space = 1

for i in range(n - 1, 0, -1):
    print(" " * space + "* " * i)
    space += 1
```

space = n - 1
for i in range(1,n+1):
    print(" " * space + "* " * i)
